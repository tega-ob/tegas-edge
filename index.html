Methodology — TEGA’S EDGE
TEGA’S EDGE is player props only. Lines: FanDuel is the primary line source — every published play carries a genuine FanDuel line captured at scan time. Edge is model probability minus breakeven probability: a probability difference, not an expected return. WNBA cards are overs only. WNBA and MLB have no historical prop-lines endpoint, so validation is mechanics checks plus live paper trading against genuine lines; genuine-line archival has been automatic since 2026-09-23. Recommendations are not wagers.
